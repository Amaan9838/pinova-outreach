# Email Signatures — When to Use What

---

## 🔴 COLD EMAIL (First Contact — Emails 1 through 6)

NO SIGNATURE. Plain text only. This is non-negotiable for deliverability.

```
— Amaan
```

That's it. No last name. No title. No company. No links.
A person, not a business card.

---

## 🟡 AFTER THEY REPLY (Warm Conversation — When They Said "Show Me" or Asked a Question)

Switch to this. It's professional but not heavy. Signals you're legit without screaming "I'm trying to impress you."

```
—
Amaan Sheikh
Founder & Creative Director
Premium Web Design for Real Estate Professionals
pinova.in
```

**Why this works:**
- "Founder & Creative Director" = authority without being corporate
- "Premium Web Design for Real Estate Professionals" = niche expertise (they're a real estate professional, you specialize in them)
- One link only (your site) — clean, not needy

---

## 🟢 PROPOSALS, INVOICES & FORMAL EMAILS (After a Call, Sending a Contract)

Full signature. They already know you, now you reinforce the brand.

```
—
Amaan Sheikh
Founder & Creative Director, Pinova

Website Design & Brand Strategy for Real Estate Teams

🌐  pinova.in
✉️  amaan@pinova.in

Designing premium digital experiences for real estate 
professionals across the US.
```

---

## 🔵 AFTER YOU HAVE RESULTS (Once You've Delivered 2-3 Projects)

Add social proof to your signature. This is the most powerful version — it does selling work passively.

```
—
Amaan Sheikh
Founder & Creative Director, Pinova
pinova.in

Recent: Redesigned websites for agents in Dallas, Miami & Scottsdale
"The best investment I've made in my brand this year." — Top Producer, TX
```

**Update the cities and quote as you get real clients and real testimonials.**

---

## The Hierarchy (Never Skip a Level)

```
Cold Email         →  "— Amaan"
They Reply         →  Amaan Sheikh, Founder & Creative Director
Proposal/Invoice   →  Full signature with company + links
After Results      →  Full signature + social proof
```

---

## One More Thing: Get a US Virtual Number

For $3-5/month, get a Google Voice or Skype number with a US area code.
This does two things:
1. Makes you reachable in their timezone without international calling friction
2. A US number in your signature removes any "offshore" concern instantly

Add it to your warm/proposal signatures:

```
📱  +1 (512) 555-XXXX
```

Pick an area code from a real estate market you're targeting (Austin = 512, Miami = 305, Dallas = 214, etc.)

## HTML Signatures

The HTML versions of these signatures are in:
`email-signatures/signature-warm.html`

Open that file in your browser to preview. Copy the HTML into:
Gmail → Settings → See all settings → General → Signature → Paste

Use the HTML version for Gmail. Use the plain text versions for cold outreach tools (Instantly, Smartlead, etc.)
