📋 How to Use These:

Cold Emails (First Contact): Do NOT use any of these. Sign off as just:

No links, no HTML, no signature. Plain text only. This is critical for deliverability.

After They Reply: Switch to Variant 1 (The Authority). Clean, professional, establishes credibility without being heavy.

Proposals & Invoices: Use Variant 2 (The Premium). More formal, includes the company name and tagline.

After You Have Clients: Use Variant 3 (The Proof). Social proof in the signature is subtle but powerful — they see other agents trust you.

To install in Gmail: Settings → See all settings → General → Signature → Paste the HTML.
